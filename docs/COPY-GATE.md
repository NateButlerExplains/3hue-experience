# Copy gate

The research sections in each door panel (What creates urgency, The gap to close, Services on this path, Proof on record, How it runs, Where to start) are public buyer-facing claims. Before they go live, Nate receives one sheet per door: the rendered panel at 1440×900 plus the plain text with each figure's source. Until his OK the sections stay in the manifest with `"gate": "pending"` and the panel renders only the spec fields (promise, audience, triggers, tension, gap, services, route, decision).

Rules the copy already follows: nothing outside the claims library; no prices; no customers beyond `vocabulary.allowedCustomers`; each statistic prints its source inline; the founder's story is not on any surface; `3HUE` is never expanded.

## The guided tour (O10/O11)

The tour's script, its Ask AiVRIC answers, its summary lines and its interface words are public copy too. They go to Nate as one sheet, `docs/copy-sheets-tour.md`, generated by `node tools/tour-text.mjs --sheet` from `content/tour.json` and `content/experience.json` (it refuses to write while the lint reports an error). The sheet opens with three decisions (the first-service pairings, the use of Forcing Function and Message Stack material in public lines, and the A-LIGN 80% statistic), then lists every line as the visitor meets it, with its source, status and hash, every choice, every Ask answer, the summary, the interface words from `content/experience.json`, and what was left out on purpose.

- Until the OK, `tour.gate` stays `"pending"`: the walk button walks as it did at 7fa92e4, nothing of the tour is fetched, and only `?tour=1` shows it.
- The OK names the sheet's line hashes. A changed word changes its hash, so a later edit needs a fresh sheet and OK (and a re-render of that line's voice).
- On the OK: record it below, set `tour.gate` to `"approved"` and the new `strings.walk` label, and follow the go-live steps in `docs/DEPLOY.md`.
- **Nate's OK is the gate.** The tour sheet needs his approval alone: the 3HUE owner's review is not
  required for it, and the branch does not wait on one. (The 2026-09-10 door-panel row below records
  an owner review because that is what happened then, not because the gate requires it.)
- The voice is rendered only from signed words (`docs/VOICE.md`); a three-line trial for the listening check may come first.
