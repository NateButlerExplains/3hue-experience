## Win Trust: Prove you are ready.

**Buyer label:** SaaS & AI vendors  
**Lede:** The business is ready to grow, but proof is scattered across policies, systems, and people.  
**Audience:** For teams that need customer assurance, readiness evidence, or a faster path through diligence.

### What creates urgency

> Your buyer isn't asking whether you're secure. They're asking whether you can prove it by Friday.  
> — The Forcing Function §06, approved opening line (approved-copy)

- An enterprise deal or renewal is blocked by assurance requirements.
- A customer, insurer, or investor is asking for evidence you cannot assemble quickly.
- AI adoption is creating new customer questions about ownership and control.

61% were required to hold a certification to win or renew a contract.  
*Source: Secureframe, 2026 Cybersecurity & Compliance Benchmark (verified)*

### The gap to close

The gap is between having good intentions and being able to show repeatable evidence when the buyer asks.

68% of companies have one or fewer full-time security people, while 93% call security a top priority.  
*Source: Secureframe, 2026 Cybersecurity & Compliance Benchmark (verified)*

## Stay Ready: Operate defensibly.

**Buyer label:** Regulated operators  
**Lede:** The organization cannot afford to discover ownership or evidence gaps during an examination or incident.  
**Audience:** For regulated and risk-heavy operators that need clear ownership, resilience, response, and evidence that survives scrutiny.

### What creates urgency

> Your controls passed in December. The question is what they were doing in March.  
> — The Forcing Function §06, approved opening line (approved-copy)

- An examiner request, audit finding, or remediation deadline is active.
- An incident or continuity concern exposes a readiness gap.
- Regulatory, privacy, or AI adoption pressure is crossing operational boundaries.

72% still rely on periodic assessments; only 28% monitor continuously.  
*Source: RegScale, 2026 State of Continuous Controls Monitoring (verified)*

### The gap to close

The gap is between documented plans and an operating model that stays ready when conditions change.

86.8% of small businesses have deployed MFA, but only 51.1% require it across all key accounts.  
*Source: National Cybersecurity Alliance with CISA, 2026 (verified)*

### Services on this path

- **Cyber incident response programs**: Cyber-Incident Response Plan Development · IR Playbook Development · Cyber-IR Tabletop Exercise (TTX) · Cyber-IR Operations Command
- **Business continuity and operational resilience**: Business Continuity Plan Development · Operational Resilience Program Development
- **Managed detection and response**: Managed Detection & Response — Complete · MXDR Starter
- **Privacy management and data protection**: Managed Privacy Program · Regulator Liaison & DSAR Escalation Support · Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

### Proof on record

Engagements are scoped and delivered by the same practitioner, whose background includes closing FDA 483 observations and remediating bank Matters Requiring Attention under sustained examination.  
*Basis: 3HUE approved 100-word boilerplate (approved-copy)*

### How it runs

1. **Snapshot** Named framework crosswalks in the examiner's language: NIST CSF and 800-53/171 as the spine, mapped to ISO 27001, COBIT, FFIEC, and SOC 2.
2. **Build** Continuous controls monitoring that replaces point-in-time testing.
3. **Operate** Remediation that closes findings and keeps them closed, with board-ready risk translation.

*Source: The Forcing Function §02, What 3HUE sells them (approved-copy)*  
Pillars: Operated, not advised · Provable, continuously · Founder-delivered
