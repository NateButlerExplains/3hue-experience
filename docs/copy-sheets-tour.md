## How to read this sheet

- Text appears as the visitor sees it, with names filled in from the manifest. A line in `code` is filled in the browser from the visitor's own answers.
- **Status values:**
  - **approved-copy, verified, substantiated:** the manifest's existing statuses.
  - **adapted:** approved wording, shortened or re-voiced as AiVRIC. Where the source had figures, vendor names or comparisons with other firms, they are taken out.
  - **proposed:** new linking copy.
  - **derived:** assembled from manifest fields.
- A **ref** line quotes a manifest field word for word, with the source that field already prints.
- **Sources** are written as plain citations, because Ask shows each answer's source to the visitor. Review notes live in this sheet, not in the sources.
- The **hash** is the first 16 hex characters of SHA-256 over the text. Voice files are checked against it, so sign-off covers these exact words.
- **Callout tiles** carry each figure on screen with its printed source. No panel opens during the tour.

## Left out on purpose, and open items

- **The A-LIGN 80% statistic.** See decision 3.
- **CMMC.** The Message Stack marks it TIME-SENSITIVE (Phase II suspended 13 July 2026; re-verify before use). None of the three rooms leads with it, so Ask has no CMMC answer. Add one only with a re-check date.
- **Hiring figures.** The CISO-to-business ratio (claims library, verified) and the salary range (Message Stack §06) are left out of the "Why not just hire a CISO?" answer. A typed figure cannot enter the script: it would need a sourced manifest field. The salary range would also read as a price.
- **The owner's phrase** "so the first real incident isn't the first rehearsal" is not borrowed. `sr-incident-2` is our own wording. Borrow it only with the owner's written OK.
- **The owner's arrival question** "Who am I walking with today?" is not used. The lobby asks "Which door is yours?", and the answers are the three buyer segments.
- **Each room's emphasised stages** are not spoken on the path. The manifest holds them as stage ids (`doors[].maturityEmphasis`). A hand-typed sentence would drift from them, so the tower lights them instead.
- **Pronunciation.** No line has a `say` override yet. Check 3HUE, AiVRIC, SOC 2, NIST CSF, 800-53/171 and CloudSignals+RiskOps in the listening report before the full render.
- **When Ask finds no match** it shows `strings.tourAskNone` from `content/experience.json` (“I don't have an approved answer for that yet. The team does.”), followed by the Talk to our team link and the suggested questions. It is shown and read by screen readers, not voiced, and it is signed with the interface words above.
- **Option labels.** “See the details” (`strings.tourDetails`) and “Send me a summary” (`strings.tourSummary`) come from the interface words, so the tour and the engine share one wording. The rest (“Show me everything”, “Nobody's asking yet”, “Who signs off on this?”, “Take me to the next door”, “Where do we start?”, “The person who signs” and others) are the script's own new copy, marked "new" in the choice tables.
